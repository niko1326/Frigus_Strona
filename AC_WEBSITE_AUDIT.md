# Unified Aesthetic & Premium Installation Audit (Residential & Small Business)

## 1. Executive Summary & Realignment
After re-evaluating the project workspace (`HeroSection.astro`, `ServicesSection.astro`, `HowItWorksSection.astro`, `CTABand.astro`, and `kontakt.astro`), the positioning strategy has been realigned. We are targeting homeowners and small local businesses (boutiques, salons, clinics, offices) who share identical purchasing behaviors: they care deeply about aesthetics, clean installation, fast turnaround, and transparent pricing with a VAT invoice option.

The current messaging is slightly too generic ("montaż klimatyzacji"). We need to shift from a "handyman" perception to a "Premium Professional Installer" who guarantees a spotless, aesthetically pleasing result.

## 2. The Unified CRO Reality Check

### Positioning: Premium Precision over Generic Handyman
- **Current State:** Generic phrasing like "mounting AC units in apartments and houses" lacks the premium touch that reassures clients about their pristine living rooms or client-facing salon walls.
- **The Fix:** We must emphasize **"Aesthetics, Speed & Peace of Mind."** The copy should highlight dust-free drilling (wiercenie z odsysaniem pyłu), aesthetic line-hiding (estetyczne maskowanie instalacji), and vibration-dampened outdoor units. This reassures both homeowners and boutique business owners that their space will not be ruined during installation.

### Frictionless Lead Generation: High-Intent & Visual
- **Current State:** CTAs rely on open-ended "Zadzwoń, pogadamy" (Call, we'll talk) or a generic form. 
- **The Fix:** We need actionable, low-friction, high-reward CTAs. Instead of just a phone number, offer: "Wyślij zdjęcie ściany – wycena w 15 minut" (Send a photo of the wall – quote in 15 mins). Ensure any contact form or process explicitly mentions the ease of getting a "Faktura VAT 23%" for business expenses or 8% for residential.

### Visual & Layout Friction
- **Current State:** Missing emphasis on the actual *process* of keeping the client's home or office clean.
- **The Fix:** Highlight the physical protection of the space (floor mats, foil for furniture, industrial vacuums) as a core feature of the service, not just an afterthought.
